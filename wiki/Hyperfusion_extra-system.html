(Superheat) Our civilian researchers have discovered how to move units to another system whose coordinates we would have. The experiment almost worked with a first prototype, but more funds are needed to carry out this research.

Our engineers are waiting for the necessary funds for the civilian program to improve travel.

Warning: we will probably need an effective radar giving us a broader vision than our system before making this kind of trips.
